# What counts as interesting

## Include
- **Agent skills**: `SKILL.md` skills or collections for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, etc.
  Favor ones that do one job well and show a demo.
- **MCP servers** that connect agents to a real tool or data source.
- **Agent frameworks and harness tooling**: orchestration, memory, sandboxes,
  observability, evals, multi-agent setups.
- **Breakout repos** in general dev tooling: under ~30 days old and gaining stars fast
  (roughly 500+ in a week, or 2,000+ in a month).
- Notable releases from known orgs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Vercel, Expo, etc.) even if smaller.

## Signals that raise a repo
- Fast star growth relative to age.
- A real owner (history, other repos), working README, license, recent commits.
- Something new: a new pattern, not another wrapper of the same idea.

## Filter out (or list under "Watch out")
- **Star-farmed rings**: many near-identical repos from throwaway accounts created the
  same day, often with no code language detected.
- Piracy, cracked software, game-ROM recompilations of commercial titles, "0day" bypasses.
- Awesome-lists with no curation, and repos that are only a link to a paid product.
- Anything already covered in the last 7 days, unless it had a major release.

## Tone
Skimmable. One line of "why it matters" per item. Links on every repo.
